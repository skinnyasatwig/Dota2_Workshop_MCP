# Ordered remaining backlog

Items are ordered by leverage. “Human” means the MCP should not guess.

1. **Richer world geometry and reusable dressing.** Flat or per-corner-sloped convex/concave solid extrusions, flat/sloped convex gameplay volumes, segmented player-blocking walls, rectangular and profile-driven arches, checked navigation surfaces, bridge decks/approaches, regular rings, unequal-outline single-hole platforms, and strictly proven independent multi-hole platforms are safe. The practical ring case is compiler- and GridNav-proven; unequal single-hole boundaries, profile arches, and a two-hole partition are compiler-proven through checked composition. Top, bottom, and side material roles plus U/V texture scale, shift, bounded rotation, and object-local shared planar alignment are deterministic, fully drift-checked, and compiler-proven. Repeatable `staticPropSet` dressing places only path-safe model resources, exposes bounded uniform/XYZ scale, requires explicit collision intent, participates in whole-map model preflight, and fails closed when a checked `vphysics` model lacks real PHYS evidence. Five deterministic visual-only palettes cover 20 installed and compiler-proven variants, with CRC-gated bounds and browser galleries. The first deliberately small `animatedPropSet` library now covers symmetric team banners: two exact models, four proven looping sequences, non-solid/nav-neutral generation, animated browser previews, map-preview footprints, and real converter/compiler proof. Next, add a bounded engine visual-motion fixture that can compare two correlated frames and shut down automatically; expand the recipe library only after that confirms runtime playback. True global-world projection, curves, and freeform art remain visual geometry rather than checked graybox surfaces. Multi-hole inputs may fail closed when the candidate triangulation is nonconforming. The bridge fixture also proved that same-X/Y points at different heights alias in GridNav; do not promise independent stacked bridge and underpass routes unless a different Valve-supported representation is found.
2. **Native HUD drift (Maintenance).** The camera bridge checks several known minimap panel ids and accepts an explicit rectangle fallback. Add newly observed Valve ids only from real evidence rather than guessing.
3. **Machine-specific direct-launch compatibility (Research).** The autonomous Steam tools launch/load/test/shutdown sequence is proven. Revisit direct-launch NVIDIA behavior only after a Valve/NVIDIA change or new evidence; do not burn retries on an unchanged condition.

Gameplay redesign of the 3v3 map is deliberately outside this backlog. The map is an integration test here, not a reason to invent mechanics.
