A reusable dry-run-first `map_engine_animation_test` now owns source/model preflight, compilation, one guarded launch, state-7 readiness, exact cycle sampling, optional strict pixel proof, console scanning, reporting, and shutdown. True global-world projection, curves, and freeform art remain visual geometry rather than checked graybox surfaces. Multi-hole inputs may fail closed when the candidate triangulation is nonconforming. The bridge fixture also proved that same-X/Y points at different heights alias in GridNav; do not promise independent stacked bridge and underpass routes unless a different Valve-supported representation is found.
